    Free: the 5-minute car reset

    1. Take out trash and anything that doesn't live in the car.
    2. Wipe the steering wheel, dash, and cup holders.
    3. Brush out the vents and seat seams.
    4. Wipe the inside of the windshield and your phone mount.
    5. Clip in a fresh scent and crack the windows for a minute.

    Works with any supplies, not just our kit.
