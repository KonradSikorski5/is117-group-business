# Exit Fresh

**A small interior car-care kit that comes with a scent.** It fits in the glovebox: cleaning wipes, a microfiber cloth, a detail brush, and a vent-clip air freshener in the scent you pick.

IS117-005 group business project · Team: Konrad Sikorski (KonradSikorski5) and Safwaan (safwaanarif) · Research checkpoint, Nov 16, 2026

## Research question

**Who would buy a small interior car-care kit with a scent, and what do they need to see before they buy it online?**

## The one workflow we are designing for

**Build your kit and check out:** pick a scent → see the full price (including shipping) → add to cart.

The kit is meant to sell online first. Gas stations, auto parts stores, and campus stores are a later option. This is a plan, not something we have tested.
